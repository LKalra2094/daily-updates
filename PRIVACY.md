# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

This is a personal project. It is used by one person - its author - to send
themselves a daily summary of their own habit and health data.

## What it does

The application reads the author's own data from services the author has
authorised, including sleep records from the Google Health API. It uses that
data to generate a short daily summary message, which is delivered to the
author's own private messaging account.

## What it collects

Nothing from anyone else. The application has no users other than its author,
no sign-up, no accounts, and no public interface. It does not collect data from
visitors, because there are no visitors.

## Where data goes

Data is retrieved directly from the authorising provider's API and stored on a
private server controlled by the author. It is not sold, shared, published, or
transmitted to any third party, other than the API providers necessary to
operate the application.

## Retention

Habit and health records are retained on the author's private server for as long
as the author finds them useful, and deleted at the author's discretion.
